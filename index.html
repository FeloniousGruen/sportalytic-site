What we do

We tell sport's most remarkable stories through numbers. Often we publish them ourselves. Just as often we help other people bring their own stories and ideas to life.

01

Reels

Vertical video, as long as the story needs — twenty seconds if that's all it takes, a couple of minutes if there's more in it. Hook, argument, a number you remember.

  • Script and storyboard
  • Sourcing and checking
  • Final MP4 and a caption

Carousels & stills

Finding the story

You don't need data to start. Tell us the sport, the team or the subject and we'll go looking — for a record, a pattern, or something nobody has noticed yet.

  • Research and story hunting
  • Works with your data or without
  • Numbers or narrative

Those three are a starting point rather than a menu. We're happy to make other kinds of visuals — a different format, something longer, a one-off graphic for print or a deck. Ask and we'll tell you whether we can do it well.

Who we work with

Anyone covering sport who wants a story told properly, whether or not they've got the numbers to hand.

Creators & media

Other accounts and newsrooms covering sport. Happy to collaborate on something we both post, or to create for you the piece you've got the idea for but not the time — yours to publish, under your own name.

Teams, clubs & rights holders

Clubs, federations, races and tournaments. There's usually something in a season's records that supporters would pass on if anyone drew it for them. We'll make it for your channels, the same as we would for a creator.

Data & tech companies

If the data is the product, the charts around it are doing the selling. We make cleaner ones for launches, reports and the feed.

How it works

If something happens tonight and it needs to be out tonight, we can do that. More often it's a few days of going back and forth about what makes the most compelling viewing, which is usually what makes the difference.

Find the story

Bring a dataset, a subject, or just a hunch. We go looking and come back with the one or two things worth telling.

Check it

Every figure traced to a source we can name on screen. If it won't survive a reply from a statistician, it doesn't go out.

Mock up a draft

We put together a rough version and then talk it through with you — what lands, what drags, what to cut.

Render and hand over

Vertical for the feed, plus whatever other sizes you need, and a caption written to go with it.
